Intro

I teach people how to make videos. Most of what I teach has very little to do with cameras. It's the harder part: sitting with an idea long enough to turn it into something worth watching.

Now

Most of my week goes to Aevy Video School. I build the curriculum, I teach, and I spend a lot of time answering questions from people who are further along than they think they are.

It sits alongside AevyTV, the channel the whole thing grew out of, and GenC, a five-month creator school in Bangalore where I teach in a room instead of on a call. The room changes how people learn more than I expected it to.

Aevy has grown into the biggest thing I've built, and I still find that hard to say out loud without flinching.

Otherwise

The rest of the time I'm at my computer, chasing whatever I got curious about that morning. Most of it goes nowhere. Some of it becomes the next thing I teach.
